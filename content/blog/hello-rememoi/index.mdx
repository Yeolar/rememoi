Every personal site begins with a small act of optimism: the belief that something is worth writing down.

This is mine. It is called **Rememoi** — a soft collision of *remember* and *moi*. Not a productivity system, not a second brain, not a brand. Just a shelf for the ideas, images and small discoveries that I want to keep within reach.

## Why another blog?

Most of what I make disappears into chats, notes apps and the long tail of browser tabs. A blog is slower than that, and that is precisely the point. Writing something down in public forces a kind of honesty: the sentence has to stand on its own.

<Aside type='tip'>

The site is designed for reading first. No pop-ups, no newsletter modal, no infinite feed. If a page takes a little longer to load because it is busy setting a beautiful code block, that is a trade I am happy to make.

</Aside>

There is also a technical reason. I wanted a place to experiment with the web platform without shipping those experiments into someone else's product. This site is a small, safe laboratory.

## What you will find here

- Notes on **code and design**, often about the seams between them.
- Small essays about **music, typography and attention**.
- References and experiments I want to remember later.
- The occasional unfinished thought, marked as a draft and hidden from the public until it is ready.

<Steps>

1. Write something down before it evaporates.
2. Edit it until the shape feels natural.
3. Publish it, then resist the urge to rewrite it immediately.
4. Come back a month later and see what still holds.

</Steps>

## How this site is built

The visual language comes from [Astro Theme Pure](https://github.com/cworld1/astro-theme-pure), a beautifully restrained theme by CWorld. The original is written in Astro; this version is a careful Next.js port, with a small admin editor and a database behind it.

<Collapse title='A few implementation notes'>

- The public pages are server components, statically rendered and revalidated when a post changes.
- Markdown and MDX are rendered with a unification pipeline that includes GFM, KaTeX and Shiki.
- The admin area is protected by GitHub OAuth (or a local development login).
- Images uploaded through the editor are stored in the database and served from an immutable media route.

</Collapse>

The result should feel like the original theme, with a few extra conveniences that only matter to the person writing.

## A small promise

I will not pretend to have a publishing schedule. Some months will have three posts; others will have none. The goal is not volume. The goal is to keep the habit warm.

<Spoiler>I have a folder of half-written posts that would suggest otherwise.</Spoiler>

> The things we remember become the shape of who we are. Choose them carefully, and then let them be light.

Thanks for stopping by. If something here is useful, or wrong, or worth arguing with, the door is open.
